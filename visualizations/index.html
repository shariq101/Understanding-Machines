Read the limits. Ablation removes a component’s image-specific write, not the component itself. CKA compares geometry, not computation. A small KL is not proof that a block is unnecessary, and a high CKA is not proof of causal transfer. The microscope ranks correlations in a ten-class image bank, not causal roles in full ImageNet.

The underlying lab scripts, checkpoints, notebooks, and captured outputs live in the repository’s labs/ folder. The source measurements and run commands are documented in the repository README.
