How to read this view

Each image is ranked by the maximum response of this channel anywhere in its spatial map. The number is that maximum; the heatmap is the same channel’s spatial response, rescaled for display. The top-class chips count labels among these five strongest images. This is evidence about what fires the channel, not a causal circuit and not a claim that the channel has only one job.

What this is. Four hooked InceptionV1 layers, measured with torchvision GoogLeNet on Imagenette2-320. All 512 channels of inception4c are available; the other layers carry their strongest selected channels.

What this is not. The image bank has ten coarse classes, not ImageNet’s full 1,000. The checkpoint is the standard torchvision GoogLeNet, not Goodfire’s architecture-modified model. A top image is a correlation with the channel, not proof of a causal role.
